About Mushavo

Property management works better when responsibility is clear.

Mushavo is being built for the real relationships behind property operations: the owner who remains accountable, the tenant whose identity remains their own, the manager acting with permission, and the staff member responsible for specific work.

Our mission

Make property operations easier to trust.

Bring people, agreements, money, maintenance, and documents into one clear operating record.

Our vision

Professional tools for every portfolio size.

Support the individual landlord and the property company without losing clarity as teams and portfolios grow.

Our promise

Keep access and ownership distinct.

Delegation should make work easier without making responsibility or historical records ambiguous.

Why Mushavo exists

The work is connected. The tools should be too.

A property can generate leases, payments, deposits, maintenance, documents, approvals, and years of history. When those records live in unrelated places, everyday work slows down and accountability becomes harder.

Less work lost in conversations

Requests and decisions belong with the relevant property, unit, lease, payment, or maintenance record rather than disappearing inside personal messages.

Clearer delegated management

Independent managers and property companies can operate approved landlord portfolios while each owner’s context and permissions stay separate.

Financial records with meaning

Rent, refundable deposits, maintenance charges, and other payments are different business events and should not be treated as one balance.

History that survives change

People move, contracts end, and management relationships change. Legitimate lease and payment history should remain available to those authorised to retain it.

Principles behind the platform

Rules that protect the working relationship.

These principles shape how Mushavo approaches accounts, access, property data, and the records created through daily operations.

01

The landlord remains the owner of the property record.

Managers can receive meaningful operating access, but delegated access does not silently transfer ownership or erase accountability.

02

The tenant owns their account identity.

A tenant can accept or reject a landlord connection and reuse their Mushavo account as their housing relationships change.

03

Permissions must follow the job.

Staff should see and do only what their assignment requires. Hiding a button is useful, but the underlying data rules must enforce the same boundary.

04

Suspension and subscription expiry are different.

An administrative suspension should not destroy the plan, history, or subscription state that existed before the account was suspended.

05

Markets need local context.

Countries can require different currencies, pricing, administration, language, and operating practices while sharing a consistent platform.

Who we serve

One platform, several points of responsibility.

Mushavo respects the differences between owning, renting, managing, administering, and completing assigned work.

Property owners

For landlords who need an organised view of properties, people, payments, maintenance, and delegated access.

Tenants

For residents who need a clear record of their lease, payments, proofs, requests, notices, and documents.

Professional managers

For independent portfolio managers and property companies coordinating work across approved owner portfolios.

Operational teams

For staff, contractors, and administrators who need focused tools, explicit assignments, and appropriate access.
