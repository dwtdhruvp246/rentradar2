Common questions

Before you choose a plan.

Does a tenant need a paid subscription?

No. Tenant accounts are independent from landlord and management subscriptions. A tenant can use their account for approved links, leases, payment records, maintenance, and documents made available to them.

What is the difference between an IPM and a PMC?

An Individual Portfolio Manager operates delegated landlord portfolios as an individual management account. A Property Management Company coordinates delegated portfolios through a company structure that can include company staff and broader team operations.

Why does pricing change by country?

Mushavo can publish plans using the currency, limits, and commercial structure appropriate to each market. Select your country above to view currently available plans.

What happens when a subscription expires?

Subscription expiry and administrative suspension are treated separately. The account’s plan and historical records remain distinct from whether access is currently active.

Can I discuss a plan for a larger portfolio?

Yes. Use the contact page and choose Sales and pricing. Include your country, account type, portfolio size, and any team or reporting requirements.
