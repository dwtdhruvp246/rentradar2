# Mushavo public website override

This page family uses the core Mushavo teal, blue, white, charcoal, and border tokens with more generous marketing-page spacing than the authenticated dashboard.

## Direction

- Deep navy hero bands communicate operational trust.
- The supplied Mushavo mark and wordmark are the primary visual assets.
- Manrope is reserved for public headings; Inter remains the body and interface font.
- The property operations flow is the signature visual: portfolio, occupancy, money, and maintenance in one connected record.
- Sections use full-width bands and ruled grids. Cards are reserved for plans, role choices, and the enquiry form.
- No invented product statistics, customer logos, testimonials, or operational data.

## Interaction

- Public navigation becomes a labelled drawer below 768px.
- Pricing filters use segmented controls and a native country select.
- Pricing is loaded from public Supabase plan records and has an honest unavailable state.
- Contact submissions use structured enquiry fields with compatibility handling for the earlier schema.
- All controls remain at least 44px high with visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
